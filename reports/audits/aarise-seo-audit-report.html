Key insight: The US market on Pharma has 14,458 impressions but only 40 clicks — a 0.28% CTR. Industry standard for this type of page is 2–3%. This single fix could bring 200–400 additional US clicks per month.
What 2026 SEO Research Says — Applied to Aarise
Information Gain is now a ranking factor — Google measures how much new useful info your content adds. Template-style country blogs score near zero and get suppressed. Real content with specs, MOQ, COA info wins.
51% of B2B buyers now start on ChatGPT/Perplexity — Content needs FAQ sections and structured data so AI tools cite it. Pharma already appears in Google AI Overviews — we need to reinforce this.
Entity SEO over keywords — Google needs to understand the site is "about" pharma APIs and research compounds. Healthcare currently signals a lifestyle/fashion site due to wrong categories.
Meta descriptions directly control CTR — Pages with no meta description show auto-generated snippets that don't convert. This explains the 0.28% US CTR on Pharma despite strong rankings.
E-E-A-T matters for pharma content — No author bios = no trust signal. For medical/pharma content Google actively checks expertise signals. Missing on both sites.
Product + capability pages beat blogs for B2B pharma — The Pharma city pages (API Supplier in Haridwar etc.) are the right strategy. They need meta descriptions and FAQ schema to convert impressions.

Week 1 Action Plan — What We Are Doing & Why
1
Fix meta descriptions — Dispersible Tablets + all US pages on Pharma
Why: The Dispersible Tablets page has 108,468 impressions and 0.41% CTR. The US market has 14,458 impressions and 0.28% CTR. A good meta description tells the searcher exactly what they'll find and why to click. This is the highest ROI task on both sites.
Expected impact: CTR increase from 0.41% → 2%+ on Dispersible Tablets = 1,500+ extra clicks/month. US CTR 0.28% → 1.5%+ = 150+ extra US clicks.

2
Fix 32 "Research Research" title typos on Healthcare
Why: Google's spam detection flags repeated words in titles as low-quality content. These 32 posts are likely being suppressed or ranked lower because of this. Fixing them signals content quality improvement.
Expected impact: These pages begin ranking properly. Impressions start growing for country-targeted content within 2–4 weeks.

3
Fix Healthcare site categories — create Peptides, Steroids, Hormones, Research Compounds
Why: Currently all 91 blogs are "Uncategorized" and the site has default WordPress tags like "Fashion" and "Trending Outfit." Google uses site taxonomy to understand what a site is about. Right now it can't tell Healthcare is a pharma site.
Expected impact: Site-wide authority improvement. Google starts associating the domain with research compound keywords. Rankings across all pages improve gradually.
