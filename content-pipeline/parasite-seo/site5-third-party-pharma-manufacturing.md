### What is Third Party Pharma Manufacturing?

Third party pharmaceutical manufacturing (also called contract manufacturing) is when a pharmaceutical company outsources the production of its products to a certified manufacturing facility. The client provides the formula and branding, and the manufacturer handles production, quality control, and packaging.

**Why companies choose third party manufacturing in India:**
- 50–70% lower production costs vs. USA or Europe
- WHO GMP certified facilities meeting international standards
- No capital investment in manufacturing infrastructure
- Faster time to market with existing capacity
- Full regulatory documentation for target market approvals
