### What to Look for in an API Supplier from India

India is the world's largest exporter of pharmaceutical APIs, supplying over 20% of global generic medicines. When sourcing APIs from India, procurement teams typically verify:

- **WHO GMP Certification** — mandatory for export to regulated markets (USA, EU, Germany, UK)
- **Certificate of Analysis (COA)** — batch-specific purity and quality documentation
- **Drug Master File (DMF)** — required for US FDA submissions
- **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)** — varies by molecule, typically 1kg–100kg for specialty APIs
- **Lead time** — standard 2–4 weeks for stocked molecules, 6–8 weeks for custom synthesis
