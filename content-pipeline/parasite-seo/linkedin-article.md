## ARTICLE TITLE:
How to Find a Reliable API Manufacturer in India (And What to Check Before You Order)

---

## ARTICLE CONTENT:

If you're a pharmaceutical company looking to source APIs from India, you already know India dominates global API supply — over 500 molecules manufactured, exported to 200+ countries.

The problem is knowing which manufacturer to trust.

After working with pharmaceutical companies across USA, Germany, Brazil, and Mexico on API sourcing, here's the checklist that actually matters:

---

**1. WHO GMP Certification — Non-Negotiable**

Any API entering a regulated market (USA, EU, Brazil, Mexico) must come from a WHO GMP certified facility. Ask for the current GMP certificate and cross-check the facility name with the issuing authority's database.

Red flag: manufacturers who can't produce the certificate within 24 hours of being asked.

---

**2. Certificate of Analysis (COA) — Request Before You Order**

A legitimate Indian API manufacturer will provide a batch-specific COA covering:
- Assay (purity %)
- Related substances / impurity profile
- Residual solvents
- Heavy metals
- Microbial limits
- Shelf life and storage conditions

If the COA is generic or not batch-specific — walk away.

---

**3. Drug Master File (DMF) — Required for US FDA Submissions**

If you're submitting an ANDA or NDA in the USA, your API supplier needs a US FDA Drug Master File (DMF). Ask for the DMF number and verify it on FDA's DMF database at fda.gov.

---

**4. Sample Before Bulk — Always**

A reliable manufacturer will send a sample (usually 100g–500g) before you commit to a bulk order. Use this to verify the COA specifications in your own lab.

---

**5. Export Experience to Your Market**

An API manufacturer exporting to Germany for the first time will face different documentation requirements than one that has done it 50 times. Ask specifically: "How many shipments have you made to [country] in the last 12 months?"
