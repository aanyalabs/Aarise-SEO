## Q1: What Blog Content Actually Ranks for Pharma B2B / API Supplier Sites?

### Core Finding: Match Procurement Intent, Not Marketing Intent

Buyers search for:
- Regulatory compliance proof: ICH Q7, ICH Q11, FDA DMF filings, GMP certificates
- Specific API names + "manufacturer" / "supplier" / "exporter" (e.g., "Metformin HCl API manufacturer India")
- Supply chain resilience content (post-COVID, buyers research Indian supplier stability)
- Pricing transparency and MOQ information

### Formats and Word Counts That Work
- Content ranking positions 1-3 averages **2,847 words**
- Technical how-to guides: 1,800–2,500 words
- Industry reports / white papers: 2,500–4,000 words
- Case studies: 1,500–2,000 words

## Q2: Does Parasite SEO Still Work in 2026?

### Short Answer: As a Primary Strategy — No. As Supplementary Signal — Selectively Yes.

**What Google Did**
- March 2024: Site Reputation Abuse introduced as manual action policy
- August 2025: Became fully algorithmic — Google evaluates sections of a domain independently
- March 2026: Further intensified with spam update
- Lifespan of spammy parasite pages: dropped from ~9 months to **6–8 weeks**
- Core mechanism broken: Google strips host domain's authority from third-party pages

### Platform-by-Platform Assessment

| Platform | Works? | Best Use for Aarise |
|---|---|---|
| Google Sites | Risky / limited | Not primary tactic; may work briefly for very low-competition queries. Treat as link passes, not ranking pages |
| **LinkedIn Articles** | Yes | B2B pharma is LinkedIn's domain. API sourcing, regulatory compliance, manufacturing capability content does rank. **Use it.** |
| Substack | Selectively | DR 91. Works for serialized newsletter-format content. "Pharma Sourcing Weekly" could build brand + occasionally rank |
| Medium | Declining | Only detailed 2,000+ word expert pieces still rank on niche queries |
| Reddit | Strong signal | Not for direct leads; but r/chemistry, r/pharmacology mentions build brand entity signals |

## Q4: How to Get AI Citations (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini)?

### The Technical Issue — Check Robots.txt First

AI crawlers that MUST be explicitly allowed in robots.txt:
- `GPTBot` (OpenAI / ChatGPT)
- `PerplexityBot`
- `Google-Extended` (Gemini / AI Overviews)
- `ClaudeBot` (Anthropic)
- `CCBot` (Common Crawl — feeds many LLMs)

If these are blocked (even by accident via Disallow: /), you will get zero AI citations.

### llms.txt — Real Talk

Place a plain-text Markdown file at `aarisepharma.com/llms.txt` and `aarisehealthcare.com/llms.txt`. It tells LLMs which pages matter most and what the site is about. **It is a signal, not a magic lever** — major LLM crawlers don't always proactively request it. Implement it, but don't expect immediate results.

### What Actually Gets You Cited

1. **Answer-first formatting** — Every page opens with a 40–60 word direct answer. LLMs extract answer blocks, not narrative paragraphs.
2. **Schema markup** — Use `Organization`, `Product`, `FAQPage`, and `HowTo` schema. AI systems prioritize structured, machine-readable content.
3. **Bing optimization** — ChatGPT has 87% citation overlap with Bing index. Submit sitemap to Bing Webmaster Tools immediately.
4. **Freshness for Perplexity** — 65% of Perplexity bot hits target content updated within the last year. Update key pages every 6 months.
5. **Original data and named facts** — "Our shipment data shows X" = citable primary source. LLMs prefer primary sources.
6. **Brand mention monitoring** — Test these 5 prompts weekly across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude:
   - "Who are the top API manufacturers in India?"
   - "Which Indian pharma companies export to the USA?"
   - "Where can I source [specific API] from India?"
   - "Best third-party pharma manufacturers India"
   - "Indian GMP-certified API supplier"

## Q5: Research Compounds / Peptide Niche — aarisehealthcare.com Strategy

### Market Size
- US peptide-related searches: 10.1 million/month by January 2026
- ~60% is weight-loss focused (Semaglutide, Tirzepatide) — highest volume, stiffest competition
- BPC-157, TB-500, Ipamorelin — lower competition, high research-buyer intent, **fastest path to ranking**

### The YMYL / E-E-A-T Challenge

Google classifies peptide/research compound content as YMYL (Your Money, Your Life). Requirements:
- Named authors with verifiable credentials (PhD, pharmacologist, biochemist)
- Content must cite peer-reviewed studies (PubMed references)
- Technical specifications must be accurate (purity %, HPLC data, COA)
- Site must clearly communicate it is a **research supplier**, not a consumer health brand

### FDA Compliance — Content Must Be Right

The FDA crackdown (late 2024 onwards) targeted companies whose total marketing context implied human use even when they had disclaimers. Required:
- Disclaimer language: "Not for human consumption, diagnostic, or potential wellness benefit"
- Disclaimer above the fold on every product page
- Never mention human benefits. Never use "treatment."
- Structure all content around: "in vitro studies show," "animal model research indicates," "laboratory applications"

### Specific Tactics for aarisehealthcare.com

**Start with lower-competition compounds:**
1. "BPC-157 research compound supplier" — high intent, manageable competition
2. "TB-500 peptide laboratory supply"
3. "CJC-1295 research grade supplier"
4. Then Semaglutide/Tirzepatide once domain authority is built

**Content format that ranks (2,000–5,000 words per product page):**
- Mechanism of action (citing PubMed studies)
- Available research applications
- Purity specifications, storage requirements, solubility data
- COA information
- Compliant disclaimer above the fold

**Blog topics that earn backlinks:**
- "BPC-157 Research Summary: 47 Published Studies Reviewed"
- "How Research Labs Should Store Lyophilized Peptides"
- "Reconstitution Protocol for [Compound Name]: Step-by-Step"
- "BPC-157 vs TB-500: Research Applications Compared"

## Q6: International SEO — Why Traffic is India-Only and How to Fix It

### Root Cause

Without hreflang tags and language-specific pages, Google defaults to showing English content to Indian users. International buyers searching in Spanish or German never see the site.

### Architecture: Use Subdirectories (Not Subdomains)

```
aarisepharma.com/es/         ← Spanish LATAM
aarisepharma.com/de/         ← German
aarisepharma.com/pt-br/      ← Brazilian Portuguese
```

Subdomains behave like separate sites and divide authority. Subdirectories keep everything on one domain — critical at this stage.

### Hreflang Implementation (Required on Every Page)

```html
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="en" href="https://aarisepharma.com/api-manufacturer/" />
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="es" href="https://aarisepharma.com/es/fabricante-api/" />
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="de" href="https://aarisepharma.com/de/api-hersteller/" />
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="pt-BR" href="https://aarisepharma.com/pt-br/fabricante-api/" />
<link rel="alternate" hreflang="x-default" href="https://aarisepharma.com/api-manufacturer/" />
```

Common mistake: translating body text but NOT translating meta titles, meta descriptions, and image alt text. Google uses these for ranking signals.

### Spanish LATAM — Market-Specific Terms

Mexico terminology: "fabricante de APIs," "materia prima farmacéutica," "maquiladora farmacéutica," "terceros laboratorios"

Brazil terminology: "fabricante de IFA" (**not** API — Brazil uses Ingrediente Farmacêutico Ativo), "terceirização farmacêutica"

→ A single Spanish page does NOT cover all LATAM. Mexico-specific Spanish and Brazil-specific Portuguese are separate pages.

### German Market

Buyers only find you if you have German-language content.

Key German keywords:
- "API Hersteller Indien" (API manufacturer India)
- "Wirkstoffhersteller" (active ingredient manufacturer)
- "Pharmazeutische Auftragsfertigung" (pharmaceutical contract manufacturing)
- "GMP-zertifizierter Lieferant Indien"

German buyers prioritize: GMP certificates, DMF filings, WHO GMP compliance, EDQM CEP status. This is the primary trust signal that converts German inquiries.

### Execution Priority

1. **Brazilian Portuguese first** — largest LATAM pharma market ($97B+, actively importing Indian APIs)
2. **Spanish/Mexico second** — largest Spanish-speaking market, active CMO importer
3. **German third** — highest per-unit value, most rigorous buyers
4. **South Korea** — start with English + Korea-specific trust signals (KFDA compliance mentions)
